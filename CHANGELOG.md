# Changelog

## 0.3.2 beta
- Add feed accepts a normal website address and finds its RSS/Atom feed (advertised link first, then /feed, /rss and similar).

## 0.3.1 beta
- Prettier news feed: large lead-story card with photo, thumbnails, date header, softer cards.

## 0.3 beta
- News feed: swipe right from the first home page for a local RSS reader with editable feeds.
- Dock holds up to 20 apps and scrolls sideways.

## 0.2 beta
- Multiple home pages; drag a widget to the screen edge to move it to another page.
- Wallpaper shows the whole picture with a blurred fill, plus a Fill option.

## 0.1 beta
- First phone release: widgets, clock, weather, dock, app drawer.
